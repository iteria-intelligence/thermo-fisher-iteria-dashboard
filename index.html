DATA CONTEXT

About this view

This dashboard uses synthetic demo data: 24 illustrative shifts, 96 activity intervals, an event map and 120 simulated cycle durations. These are not measured TFS results. Original videos and evidence images are real source material; the workflow planning assumptions are estimates.

Collection & analysis

1,000 hours is the collection program stated by the team. The chart counts and activity patterns are illustrative values for this demonstration. Coarse clip labels do not imply continuous computer input.

Activity portions

Activity portions describe the synthetic demo sample. They do not estimate activity across all 1,000 hours of raw footage.

Automation scenario

Manual setup of 3.4–6.8 minutes and assisted setup of 1.7–3.5 minutes are planning assumptions for four arrays, with paired savings of 1.7–3.3 minutes. They exclude instrument waits and thermal cycling. Savings have not been measured.

Elapsed-time references

Thermo Fisher lists about four hours for genotyping instrument runs, and about five hours from purified DNA to genotyping data. Different scopes; not additive. These do not establish the actual TFS run duration.
